Savings and protection · Plan 770

LIC's Bima Platinum

A non-participating, non-linked, limited-premium savings plan. You pay for a shorter period and receive guaranteed additions, a booster income and a regular yearly income.

Premium-paying term
7, 10, 12, 15 or 18 years
Entry age
30 days completed to 55 years, depending on the term
Minimum sum assured
₹3 lakh, in multiples of ₹10,000
Maximum sum assured
No fixed limit, subject to LIC underwriting
Key benefits
  • Guaranteed Additions of ₹70 per ₹1,000 of annual premium paid during the premium-paying term
  • Booster Income equal to 70% of the basic sum assured if you survive to the end of the premium-paying term plus 5 years
  • Regular Income equal to 10% of the basic sum assured at the end of each year of the payout period (policy term minus premium-paying term)

  • Rebate for existing LIC policyholders and for nominees of deceased policyholders
  • Optional riders available for extra premium

Good for: Savings with life cover and a planned income after the premium term

Life vs health

You usually need both.

They solve different problems. One protects your family's future income, the other protects your savings today.

Life insurance (LIC)Health insurance (Galaxy)
Pays whenThe insured passes away during the term, or the plan maturesYou are hospitalised or need covered treatment
Pays whomYour nominee, or you at maturityThe hospital, or you as reimbursement
ProtectsFamily income and long-term goalsYour savings from medical bills
RenewalPremiums over the policy termUsually renewed every year

Questions

Common questions.

How do I pay my premium?

Premiums are paid to LIC or the insurer through its official channels, such as its website and app, a branch or an authorised centre. Selvakumar will show you how and remind you before each due date.

Can I change my mind after buying?

Most life and health policies include a free-look period, usually 15 days from receiving the policy document. During that time you can review the terms and return the policy if it does not suit you.

Will a health policy cover existing conditions?

Usually only after a waiting period. The length differs by plan, so check it before you buy and disclose your health history honestly on the proposal.

How much life cover do I need?

A common starting point is a sum that replaces your family's income for many years and clears any loans. Selvakumar will work out a figure with you based on your situation.

Is there a charge for advice?

No. Advice and quotes are free. If you would like to know how an agent is paid, ask Selvakumar and he will explain.
