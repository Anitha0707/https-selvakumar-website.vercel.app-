Jeevan Lakshya

An endowment plan that pays your family an income if you pass away, and the full amount at maturity.

Get a free quote →Back to Savings and Endowment

Plan at a glance

Plan typeNon-linked, with-profits endowment
Entry age18 – 50 years
Policy term13 – 25 years
Premium paying termPolicy term minus 3 years
Minimum sum assured₹1,00,000
Annual income on death10% of basic sum assured, every year till maturity

Key benefits

  • If you pass away, your nominee receives 10% of the basic sum assured every year until the plan matures
  • At maturity, the full sum assured plus accrued bonuses is paid, even after death
  • Future premiums are waived after death and the policy continues
  • If you survive, the sum assured plus bonuses is paid at maturity
  • Tax benefits as per the prevailing Income Tax law

Best suited for

Parents planning for a child's higher education or marriage who want protection now and an income for the family if something happens.
