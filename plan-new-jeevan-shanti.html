New Jeevan Shanti

A single-premium deferred annuity that gives you an income for life.

Get a free quote →Back to Retirement

Plan at a glance

Plan typeNon-participating, non-linked single-premium deferred annuity
Entry age30 – 79 years
Deferment period1 – 5 years
PremiumSingle premium (one-time payment)
Minimum purchase price₹1,50,000
Annuity payoutMonthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly

Key benefits

  • Choose single life, or joint life with a spouse, sibling or lineal family member
  • A guaranteed income for life after the deferment period
  • On death, your nominee receives the higher of 105% of the purchase price or the purchase price plus accrued benefit, less annuity paid
  • A loan is available three months after issue, up to 80% of the surrender value
  • Annuity income is taxed as per your income slab

Best suited for

People at or near retirement with a lump sum who want a guaranteed lifetime income with no market risk, and accept less flexibility in return.
